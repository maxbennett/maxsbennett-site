Max Bennett

PhD candidate, Department of Computer Science, Columbia University

max.bennett@columbia.edu · CV · Google Scholar · X · LinkedIn

I am a PhD candidate in computer science at Columbia University, advised by Rich Zemel. I work on neural memory and continual learning — in particular, memory systems for language models that can be instructed, in natural language, what to learn and what to retain. Before this, my research was in evolutionary neuroscience, asking what the 600-million-year history of brains can tell us about building intelligent machines.

I also cofounded Bluecore, where I am Chief Product Officer, and Alby AI, acquired by Bluecore in 2024. I serve on the board of the Thousand Brains Project.
